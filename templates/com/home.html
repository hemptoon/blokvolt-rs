How we check

  1. Public sources only.Company websites, operators' price lists and apps, statistics offices, regulators, funds and law texts. Where only the media or a dealer gives a figure, we mark it as medium or low reliability.
  2. A date on everything.Each figure shows the date on its source and the date we checked it. Markets change quickly; an old price is labelled as old.
  3. Same rules for every company.We copy what a company says on its own site — what it sells, its prices, whether it installs — into the same columns for everyone. No ratings, no rankings, no paid placements.
  4. Gaps are published.Each country page lists what we could not verify, so you know where the picture is incomplete.
