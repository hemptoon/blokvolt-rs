Listing on BlokVolt

BlokVolt lists companies that sell or install home chargers, and public charging networks, in Serbia and the Western Balkans. A listing is free. We copy what a company says on its own website into the same columns for everyone — what it sells, published prices, whether it installs, where it works — with the date we checked it. There are no ratings, no rankings and no paid placements.

To add your company or correct a detail, send the company name, website and city — or the correction with a link to where it is published — to {{ EMAIL }}. Serbian companies can also use the correction page on blokvolt.rs.

Charging hardware suppliers

The installation business behind BlokVolt now operates as Evolako and works in Serbia. Quotations, datasheets and partnership offers for wallboxes, sub-meters and accessories go to hello@evolako.com.

Being a supplier does not change a listing. Whether a company sells to Evolako has no effect on whether or how it appears on BlokVolt: every company is shown by the same public rules.
